Step 10 Stabilization Phase

Embody Your New Daily State

This is not a step you complete once. This is the practice you carry forward from here, with every decision, every day.

The Teaching

You have now entered the Stabilization phase. This portion of the framework is not about completing a task and moving on. It is about stabilizing your thoughts, feelings, intentions, energy, and frequency so that your new timeline can snap into place. Physical reality takes time to catch up with energetic shifts. That window can be hours, weeks, or months depending on what you are shifting and how consistently you hold the new frequency.

Step 10 builds directly on what you did in Step 9. You have your action steps. Now the practice expands: with every significant decision you face going forward, you run it through one filter. What would the version of me already living this timeline choose? Let the answer rise intuitively. Then do that thing.

This also means following the breadcrumbs. You asked your spirit team for help. They are delivering. When an intuitive nudge arrives, when inspiration appears unprompted, when an opportunity shows up that feels aligned, these are responses to your declaration. Act on the ones that feel soul-aligned, even if they do not look exactly like what you expected.

This Is an Ongoing Practice

You will have access to a daily Timeline Jumping practice in this phase that gives you specific tools to support your stabilization. The intuition development toolkit is also available here to help you get better at recognizing and trusting the nudges that your divine team sends through.

For now, the most important thing is the filter. Before significant decisions, pause and ask: what would this version of me choose? Tolerate? Decline? What are they saying yes to? What are they saying no to? You are no longer the person who lived the old timeline. You are simply becoming more fully the person who lives the new one.

Before Each Decision

Take a breath and settle into the version of you who already lives this reality. Not who you are trying to become. Who you already are, on the timeline you have selected.

The Decision Filter

These are the questions to hold as you move through your days. You do not need to answer all of them right now. Read through them, let them land, and return to them whenever a decision is in front of you.

Choose
What would the version of me already living this timeline choose here?
Tolerate
What does this version of me no longer accept or put up with?
Say Yes To
What opportunities, offers, or invitations would they say yes to?
Say No To
What would they decline, release, or walk away from without guilt?
Follow
What intuitive nudge or breadcrumb have I been getting that I have not acted on yet?
Embody
How does this version of me show up today, in how I speak, act, and carry myself?
